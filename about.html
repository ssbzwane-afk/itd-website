About ITD

Creating opportunities that empower individuals, strengthen families and transform communities.

Who We Are

The Institute for Transformative Development (ITD) is a South African non-profit organisation committed to creating opportunities through education, skills development, mentorship and community partnerships.

We believe that every individual deserves access to quality education, meaningful employment opportunities and the support required to realise their full potential.

Our Vision

To build thriving communities where every person has access to learning opportunities, meaningful work, dignity and the ability to contribute positively to society.

Our Mission

To empower individuals through education, mentorship, skills development and strategic partnerships that create sustainable pathways to employment and community prosperity.

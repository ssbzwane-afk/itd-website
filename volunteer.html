Become a Volunteer

Share Your Skills. Change Lives.

Volunteers are at the heart of our work. Whether you have a few hours each month or can commit regularly, your contribution can make a lasting impact.

Why Volunteer?

Volunteering with ITD allows you to make a meaningful difference while developing valuable experience and building strong community relationships.

Volunteer Opportunities

Academic Tutor

Support learners with homework, revision and subject tutoring.

Career Mentor

Guide young people with career planning, CV writing and interview skills.

Administration

Assist with office administration, record keeping and event coordination.

Communications & Media

Help create content, manage social media and promote ITD's activities.

Events Support

Assist with workshops, community events and outreach activities.

Professional Skills

Share your expertise in business, finance, education, HR or technology.

Who Can Volunteer?

  • University and college students
  • Working professionals
  • Retired professionals
  • Community members
  • Corporate volunteer teams
